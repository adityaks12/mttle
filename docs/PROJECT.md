# mttle, the idea

## What mttle is
A hyperlocal introduction and matching service that connects people with the personal trainers who actually fit what they need. Today that means women finding female trainers who train them well; the same model extends to men, and to well-qualified male trainers who know how to train women's bodies and specific conditions, over time. The geography, gender focus and categories described elsewhere in this document are today's execution choices, not a permanent definition of what mttle is.

In practice: a customer browses trainer profiles for free, then pays a fee to unlock a chosen trainer's direct contact and arranges sessions with the trainer herself. Where a society gym exists, the trainer trains her there, where the equipment already is and the semi-public setting makes trust easier.

## Target
Women living in large gated societies that have a clubhouse gym, skewing young families, in the Koramangala and HSR Layout corridor to start. This is the current wedge, not the ceiling.

## Categories
- In scope now: general women's fitness (strength, weight loss, toning, beginners) and PCOS-friendly training. PCOS is low physical risk and carries real differentiation.
- Deferred: prenatal, postnatal, rehab, post-injury. The clinical risk is too high for a solo founder with no medical infrastructure. Revisit in a later phase with certified specialists and a client medical-clearance step.
- Framing: "experienced with PCOS-friendly training," never treating or managing a condition. mttle offers fitness, not medical care.
